The CDG standard

Trust the math.
Not the banner.

“50% off” is a claim. A good deal is evidence: a specific product, the retailer's current price, and the retailer's reference price — computed, never copied from an ad.

  • “Up to X% off” category banners
  • Percent-only announcements without a price pair
  • Coupon stacking, BOGO and membership-only pricing
  • Deal-blog claims without a matching retailer page
  • Anything older than 24 hours without a fresh check
01

Watch wide.

Specialist deal feeds, retailer sale pages and Black Friday ad coverage surface candidates. Headlines are leads, not proof — they go to a review queue, never straight to the board.

02

Check the pair.

One exact product or model number. The retailer's current price and its reference price, both on the retailer's own domain. Discount = (reference − sale) ÷ reference × 100, in exact decimal math.

03

35% or out.

Rounded down, never up. Evidence expires after 24 hours; a failed or blocked recheck doesn't renew it. An empty board beats a stale one.

ListedReference $199.00
Sale      $99.00
(199 − 99) ÷ 199 = 50.25%→ shows as 50%
CutReference $100.00
Sale      $65.10
(100 − 65.10) ÷ 100 = 34.90%→ never shown

Fine print, plainly

Questions.

Why only 35% and up?

It's high enough to filter out routine markdowns that show up all year, and low enough that real doorbusters on tools, TVs, appliances and toys still clear it. The line is hard: 34.99% is cut.

Does a 50% “verified drop” mean it's a great price?

It means the retailer's own sale price is half its own listed reference price. Retailers sometimes inflate reference prices, so this isn't a price-history audit. Compare at another store before buying anything big.

Why would the board ever be empty?

Each listing's evidence lasts 24 hours. If a recheck can't confirm the same price pair on the retailer's page — because the price changed, the item sold out, or the page blocked the check — the listing comes off rather than staying up on stale data. Recently expired items stay below the board, clearly marked as unverified.

Are these affiliate links?

No. Deal links currently carry no affiliate tracking. If that ever changes, it'll be disclosed here and in the footer.

Where are my saved deals stored?

Only in this browser, on this device. There's no account, no cookies and no analytics on this page.
